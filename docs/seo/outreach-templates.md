# Outreach email templates

Personalise the first line for every email. Send 10 per week at most.

## A. Resource link request (senior-care, caregiver, aging-services blogs)

Subject: A planning resource for [their topic] readers

Hi [Name],

I read your piece on [specific article]. Your advice on [specific point] is clear and useful.

I'm with SALT (Senior and Living Today) in Dayton, Ohio. We publish a free guide on [topic]: [article link]. If your readers would find it helpful, I'd be grateful if you'd consider adding it to your resources page.

Thank you for your time,
[Name], [Role], SALT (Senior and Living Today)
https://www.saltonlinestore.com/

## B. Guest article offer (estate-planning, elder-law, caregiver blogs)

Subject: Guest article idea: [working title]

Hi [Name],

Your readers ask about [question]. I'd like to offer a guest article titled "[working title]", written by Courtney R. Jones, founder of SALT (Senior and Living Today), who has more than 30 years in senior care.

The article would cover [3 points from the brief], with no sales pitch. One link back to a relevant resource is included in the author bio.

Would this suit your site? I can send a full draft within a week.

Thank you,
[Name], SALT (Senior and Living Today)

## C. Military family support

Subject: A readiness resource for military families

Hi [Name],

Your organisation supports families preparing for deployment. SALT publishes a planning guide for service members and families: [article link]. It covers medical preferences, finances and household tasks to organise before a deployment.

If it would help the families you serve, could it be added to your resources? We're happy to share a short description for your listing.

Thank you,
[Name], SALT (Senior and Living Today)

## D. Follow-up (send once, 7 days later, only if no reply)

Hi [Name], following up on my note below in case it got lost. No problem if it's not a fit. Thanks for your time.
