# SALT off-site SEO guide (backlinks, listings, outreach)

Goal: get real people and search engines to find SALT, and send them to the right product pages.
Owner: SALT team. Store: https://www.saltonlinestore.com/

## Rules we follow

- Only get links that a real person could use: directories, partners, guest articles, resource pages, press.
- No paid link schemes, link exchanges, comment spam or fake reviews. These can get the store penalised.
- No invented stats, testimonials, reviews or delivery promises in any outreach.
- Use the brand name "SALT (Senior and Living Today)" and the founder, Courtney R. Jones, as the entity.
- Products sold on Amazon: say "sold and shipped by Amazon" and link to the Amazon product page. Never promise Amazon delivery dates.
- Every link we ask for points to a page that is useful and has a clear purpose.

## Step 1: Fix your listings first (offline, about 2 hours)

These create the brand's identity on the web. Use exactly the same name, address, phone and website everywhere.

| Facebook page | Add the website, description and cover image. Link to the planner page. | https://www.saltonlinestore.com/pages/living-legacy-planner |
| Instagram | Bio with the website. Post the planner covers and short tips. | https://www.saltonlinestore.com/pages/living-legacy-planner |

| LinkedIn company page | Name, website, short description with the founder. | https://www.saltonlinestore.com/pages/about-us |

Keep the Amazon store page name consistent with "The Living Legacy Planner", and put the SALT website in the author or brand section if Amazon allows it.

## Step 2: Business directories (offline, about 3 hours)

Submit the same short description everywhere:

> SALT (Senior and Living Today) is a Dayton, Ohio brand of senior-care planners and resources. The Living Legacy Planner helps seniors and families record medical wishes, finances, insurance and funeral plans in one place. Founded by senior-care advocate Courtney R. Jones.

Directory types to submit to (check each one is free and relevant before submitting):
- Local business directories for Dayton and Ohio
- Senior-care and caregiver resource directories
- Estate-planning and end-of-life resource directories
- Military family resource directories (for the military edition)
- Shopify-approved app and store directories, where free

Track each submission in `docs/seo/backlink-targets.csv` (status column).

## Step 4: Outreach to partners and guest articles (offline first, then email)

Target types, in priority order:
1. Senior-care organisations, caregiver groups and aging-services blogs (ask for a resource link).
2. Military family and veteran support blogs and community pages (military edition).
3. Estate-planning and elder-law blogs (offer a guest article with the article briefs).
4. Local Dayton and Ohio community sites (ask for a local resource listing).
5. Podcasts and local media on senior planning (press pitch).

Use the templates in `docs/seo/outreach-templates.md`. Send in batches of 10 per week, personalise each one, and log replies in the CSV.

## Step 5: Reviews and proof (offline, ongoing)

- Ask real buyers, by email after delivery, to leave a review on the product page.
- Ask buyers' permission before using a story, photo or name.
- Never write reviews or testimonials ourselves.

## What to track each week

Use Google Search Console (Performance report):
1. Open Search results, set the date range to the last 28 days, then compare with the previous 28 days.
2. Check Total clicks, Impressions, Average CTR and Average position.
3. Under Pages, check the planner page, the collections and the products.
4. Under Queries, note the new search terms that bring clicks.
5. Under Links, check the number of external links pointing to the store.
6. Record one row per week in `docs/seo/weekly-tracker.csv`.

Shopify (Analytics) for sales: sessions, add-to-cart, checkouts and orders from search.

Good signs: impressions rising first, then clicks, then add-to-cart. Expect 6 to 12 weeks for new pages to get traction.
